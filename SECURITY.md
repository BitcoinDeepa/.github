# Security Policy

Bitcoin Deepa builds wallet, Lightning, and payment software. Security issues can
put people's funds at risk, so we take them seriously and ask you to report them
responsibly.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue, pull request, or Telegram message for security
reports.**

Instead, report privately in one of these ways:

- Use **GitHub → Security → "Report a vulnerability"** (private advisory) on the
  affected repository, **or**
- Email **<security@bitcoindeepa.com>** (or **<hello@bitcoindeepa.com>**).

Please include:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- Affected repository, version, or deployment, if known.

If you wish to encrypt your report, ask us for a PGP key by email first.

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement** within **3 business days**.
- An initial assessment and severity rating within **7 business days**.
- Coordinated disclosure: we will work with you on a fix and a disclosure
  timeline, and credit you if you would like.

## Scope

In scope: the source code in this organisation's repositories and the services
we operate (wallet bot, Lightning node, APIs).

Out of scope: third-party services, social-engineering, physical attacks, and
denial-of-service testing against our production infrastructure.

Please act in good faith, avoid privacy violations and service disruption, and
only test against your own accounts.

---

Thank you for helping keep Bitcoin Deepa and its users safe.
